📊 Statistical Analysis Flow Diagram

Improved methodology for TN Mahlangu's Masters Proposal — Multiplex FISH / PMA-qPCR study

flowchart TD
    A["📋 Raw Data Collected"] --> B{"Data Type?"}
    
    B -->|"🧬 PMA-qPCR
(viable counts)"| C["Descriptive Stats
Median ± IQR"]
    B -->|"🖼️ FISH Images"| D["Image Processing
(ImageJ + QuPath)"]
    B -->|"📋 Clinical Data"| E["Cohort Characteristics"]
    
    D --> F["Extract Metrics
• Abundance
• Fluorescence intensity
• Adherence score
• Spatial clustering"]
    
    C & F --> G{"Primary Research Question:
Do the two groups
(responsive vs resistant)
differ?"}
    
    G -->|"Cross-sectional
(single visit)"| H["Mann-Whitney U test
+ Benjamini-Hochberg
FDR correction"]
    G -->|"Longitudinal
(visits: 0, 8, 16, 24)"| I["Mixed-Effects Model
Group × Time interaction
Random intercept per subject"]
    
    H --> J{"Significant
difference
found?"}
    I --> J
    
    J -->|"Yes"| K["Post-hoc pairwise
comparisons
(Dunn's test)"]
    J -->|"No"| L["Report effect sizes
+ 95% confidence
intervals"]
    
    C & F --> M{"Secondary Question:
Are FISH metrics related
to viable bacterial counts?"}
    
    M --> N["Spearman rank
correlation"]
    N --> O["Partial Spearman
(controlling for
confounders)"]
    
    C & F --> P{"Exploratory:
What are the overall
community patterns?"}
    P --> Q["Principal Component
Analysis (PCA)
on all microbial vars"]
    Q --> R["Permutational MANOVA
(adonis2)
testing group separation"]
