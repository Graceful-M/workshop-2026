Choosing a Statistical Model in Biostatistics

Start at the top. Follow the arrows based on your data type and research question.

How to use this flowchart

  1. Start at the top — identify what type of outcome variable you have.
  2. Follow the arrows — each decision node asks a yes/no or multiple-choice question about your data.
  3. Check assumptions — the yellow diamond-shaped nodes remind you to verify normality, equal variance, etc.
  4. Pick the model — dark blue nodes show the recommended statistical test or model.
  5. When in doubt, go non-parametric — Wilcoxon, Mann-Whitney, Kruskal-Wallis, and Spearman are safer with small or non-normal samples.
